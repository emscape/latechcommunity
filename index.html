LA Tech Community

Coded in LA: inclusive, accessible technology education for Los Angeles.

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization offering workshops, tech talks, mentorship, and community events for learners and technologists at every skill level.

Our Mission

LA Tech Community's mission is to make technology education inclusive, accessible, and community-driven for people across Los Angeles.

We run hands-on workshops and tech talks, connect learners with volunteer mentors, and host community events where people of every background and skill level can build technical skills, share knowledge, and grow their careers in technology.

LA Tech Community is a nonprofit organization organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
